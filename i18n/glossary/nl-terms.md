## Keep in English

- Brand: You-Meet-In-A-Tavern, YMIAT
- Class and subclass names (Fighter, Lore, Archfey, …)
- Most spell titles and SRD proper names
- Publisher / product names (D&D, Tales of the Valiant, Pathfinder, …)
- Adventure title *The Wild Sheep Chase* (may add Dutch subtitle)

## Avoid

- Literal brand calques (“Je-Ontmoet-In-Een-Herberg”)
- Forced Dutch for jargon the table already says in English (class names, many spells)
- “rol” / “rollen” for a die. You throw a die (gooien); the result is a worp. “Rol” only means a role (a party role, the GM’s role).
