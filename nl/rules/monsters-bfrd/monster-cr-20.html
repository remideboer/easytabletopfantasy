Ancient Black Dragon

CR 20
Gargantuan Dragon
Armor Class 21 (natuurlijk pantser)
Max. wonden 355
Snelheid 40 ft., fly 80 ft., swim 40 ft.
Perception 25 Stealth 18
ResistantIf the dragon fails a save, it can choose to succeed instead.
Immuun acid | poisoned
Zintuigen darkvision 120 ft., keensense 60 ft.
Talen Common, Draconic

Acidic Vapors. After the black dragon uses its Acid Breath, acid clings to its mouth, throat, and nostrils for a time, evaporating as the dragon breathes. While the dragon’s Acid Breath is unavailable, acidic vapors surround it, and each creature that starts its turn within 30 voet of the dragon must succeed on a DC 21 CON save or be poisoned until the start of its next turn.

Amphibious. The dragon can breathe air and water.

Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the dragon fails a save, it can choose to succeed instead.

Acties

Multiattack. The dragon uses its Frightful Presence. It then makes one Bite attack and two Claw attacks.

Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 20 (2d10 + 9) Piercing Wonden plus 9 (2d8) Acid Wonden.

Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d6 + 9) Slashing Wonden.

Tail. Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (2d8 + 9) Bludgeoning Wonden.

Frightful Presence. Each creature of the dragon’s choice that is within 120 voet of the dragon and aware of it must succeed on a DC 18 WIS save or be frightened for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a creature’s save is successful or the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to the dragon’s Frightful Presence for the next 24 hours.

Acid Breath (Recharge 5-6). The dragon exhales acid in a 90-voet line that is 10 voet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 21 DEX save, taking 67 (15d8) Acid Wonden on a failed save, or half as many Wonden (rounded down) on a successful one.

Legendaire acties

De draak kan 3 legendaire acties nemen, gekozen uit de opties hieronder. Slechts één legendaire actie per keer, en alleen aan het einde van de beurt van een ander wezen. De draak krijgt gebruikte legendaire acties terug aan het begin van zijn beurt.

Detect. The dragon automatically detects every creature and trap within 30 voet of it, pinpointing the current location of each.

Tail Attack. The dragon makes a Tail attack.

Elemental Roar (Costs 2 Actions). The dragon roars at up to two creatures of its choice within 120 voet of it. Each target must succeed on a DC 18 CHA save or be vulnerable to Acid Wonden until the end of its next turn. A target resistant to Acid Wonden isn’t resistant to it for the duration. A target immune to Acid Wonden is still immune to it, even if the target fails the save.

Wing Attack (Costs 2 Actions). The dragon beats its wings. Each creature within 15 voet of the dragon must succeed on a DC 23 DEX save or take 16 (2d6 + 9) Bludgeoning Wonden and be knocked prone. The dragon can then fly up to half its flying speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

Talen Common, Draconic

Freezing Fog. As the young silver dragon, except it affects creatures within 30 voet of the dragon, and the save DC is 23.

Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the dragon fails a save, it can choose to succeed instead.

Acties

Multiattack. The dragon uses its Frightful Presence. It then makes one Bite attack and two Claw attacks. It can replace its Bite attack with a use of Paralyzing Breath, if available.

Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +17 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (2d10 + 10) Piercing Wonden.

Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +17 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d6 + 10) Slashing Wonden.

Tail. Melee Weapon Attack: +17 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 19 (2d8 + 10) Bludgeoning Wonden.

Frightful Presence. Each creature of the dragon’s choice that is within 120 voet of the dragon and aware of it must succeed on a DC 19 WIS save or be frightened for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a creature’s save is successful or the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to the dragon’s Frightful Presence for the next 24 hours.

Cold Breath (Recharge 5-6). The dragon exhales an icy blast in a 90-voet cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 23 CON save, taking 99 (22d8) Cold Wonden on a failed save, or half as many Wonden (rounded down) on a successful one.

Paralyzing Breath (Recharge 6). As the silver dragon wyrmling, except it is a 90-voet cone, and the save DC is 23.

Bonusacties

Change Shape. The dragon magically transforms into a Beast or Humanoid that has a challenge rating no higher than its own, or back into its true form, which is Dragon. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying transforms with it or is borne by the new form (the dragon’s choice). It reverts to its true form if it dies. In a new form, the dragon retains its Legendary Resistance trait and its Max. wonden, ability to speak, proficiencies, and INT, WIS, and CHA scores, as well as this bonus action. Its statistics and capabilities are otherwise replaced by those of the new form, except any class features or legendary actions of that form.

Pit Fiend

CR 20
Large Fiend (Devil)
Armor Class 19 (natuurlijk pantser)
Max. wonden 323
Snelheid 30 ft., fly 60 ft.
Perception 20 Stealth 18
ResistantThe pit fiend heeft voordeel op saves against spells and other magische effects.
Immuun charmed, frightened | Devilish Resilience
Zintuigentruesight 120 ft.
Talen Infernal, telepathy 120 ft.

Aura of the Devil Lord. Each hostile creature that starts its turn within 20 voet of the pit fiend must succeed on a DC 21 WIS save or be frightened until the start of its next turn. Each friendly devil within 20 voet of the pit fiend is immune to the charmed and frightened conditions and has advantage on the first attack roll it makes on each of its turns.

Devilish Resilience. The devil is resistant to Cold Wonden and to bludgeoning, piercing, and Slashing Wonden from nonmagical attacks. In addition, it is immune to Fire Wonden and Poison Wonden and to the poisoned condition.

Flaming Weapons. The pit fiend’s weapon attacks are magical. When the fiend hits with any weapon, the weapon deals an extra 4d6 Fire Wonden (included in the attack).

Magie Resistance. The pit fiend heeft voordeel op saves against spells and other magische effects.

Acties

Multiattack. The pit fiend makes one Bite attack, one Gore attack, one Mace attack, and one Tail attack, or it makes four Hurl Flame attacks. If two Hurl Flame attacks hit one creature, each creature within 10 voet of the target must make a DC 21 DEX save as the fire bursts outward from the target, taking 14 (4d6) Fire Wonden on a failed save, or half as many Wonden (rounded down) on a successful one.

Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 22 (4d6 + 8) Piercing Wonden plus 14 (4d6) Fire Wonden and 13 (3d8) Poison Wonden. The target must succeed on a DC 21 CON save or be poisoned. While poisoned in this way, the target can’t heal Wonden. The target can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

Gore. Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d8 + 8) Piercing Wonden plus 14 (4d6) Fire Wonden.

Mace. Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d6 + 8) Bludgeoning Wonden plus 14 (4d6) Fire Wonden.

Tail. Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 24 (3d10 + 8) Bludgeoning Wonden plus 14 (4d6) Fire Wonden, and the target must succeed on a DC 21 STR save or be knocked prone.

Hurl Flame. Ranged Spell Attack: +10 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. Hit: 25 (6d6 + 4) Fire Wonden.
