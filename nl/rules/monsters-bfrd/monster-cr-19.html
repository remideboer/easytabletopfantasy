Ancient Bronze Dragon

CR 19
Gargantuan Dragon
Armor Class 21 (natuurlijk pantser)
Max. wonden 314
Snelheid 40 ft., fly 80 ft., swim 40 ft.
Perception 27 Stealth 16
ResistantIf the dragon fails a save, it can choose to succeed instead.
Immuun lightning
Zintuigen darkvision 120 ft., keensense 60 ft.
Talen Common, Draconic

Amphibious. The dragon can breathe air and water.

Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the dragon fails a save, it can choose to succeed instead.

Lightning Extraction. As the young bronze dragon, except it affects creatures within 30 voet of the dragon, and the save DC is 22.

Acties

Multiattack. The dragon uses its Frightful Presence. It then makes one Bite attack and two Claw attacks. It can replace its Bite attack with a use of Repulsion Breath, if available.

Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 20 (2d10 + 9) Piercing Wonden.

Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 16 (2d6 + 9) Slashing Wonden.

Tail. Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 18 (2d8 + 9) Bludgeoning Wonden.

Frightful Presence. Each creature of the dragon’s choice that is within 120 voet of the dragon and aware of it must succeed on a DC 19 WIS save or be frightened for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a creature’s save is successful or the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to the dragon’s Frightful Presence for the next 24 hours.

Lightning Breath (Recharge 5-6). The dragon exhales lightning in a 120-voet line that is 10 voet wide. Each creature in that line must make a DC 22 DEX save, taking 88 (16d10) Lightning Wonden on a failed save, or half as many Wonden (rounded down) on a successful one.

Repulsion Breath (Recharge 6). The dragon exhales repulsion energy in a 90-voet cone. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 22 STR save or be pushed up to 45 voet away from the dragon.

Bonusacties

Change Shape. The dragon magically transforms into a Beast or Humanoid that has a challenge rating no higher than its own, or back into its true form, which is Dragon. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying transforms with it or is borne by the new form (the dragon’s choice). It reverts to its true form if it dies. In a new form, the dragon retains its Legendary Resistance trait and its Max. wonden, ability to speak, proficiencies, and INT, WIS, and CHA scores, as well as this bonus action. Its statistics and capabilities are otherwise replaced by those of the new form, except any class features or legendary actions of that form.

Legendaire acties

De draak kan 3 legendaire acties nemen, gekozen uit de opties hieronder. Slechts één legendaire actie per keer, en alleen aan het einde van de beurt van een ander wezen. De draak krijgt gebruikte legendaire acties terug aan het begin van zijn beurt.

Detect. The dragon automatically detects every creature and trap within 30 voet of it, pinpointing the current location of each.

Tail Attack. The dragon makes a Tail attack.

Elemental Roar (Costs 2 Actions). The dragon roars at up to two creatures of its choice within 120 voet of it. Each target must succeed on a DC 19 CHA save or be vulnerable to Lightning Wonden until the end of its next turn. A target resistant to Lightning Wonden isn’t resistant to it for the duration. A target immune to Lightning Wonden is still immune to it, even if the target fails the save.

Wing Attack (Costs 2 Actions). The dragon beats its wings. Each creature within 15 voet of the dragon must succeed on a DC 23 DEX save or take 16 (2d6 + 9) Bludgeoning Wonden and be knocked prone. The dragon can then fly up to half its flying speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

Ancient Green Dragon

CR 19
Gargantuan Dragon
Armor Class 21 (natuurlijk pantser)
Max. wonden 333
Snelheid 40 ft., fly 80 ft., swim 40 ft.
Perception 25 Stealth 17
ResistantIf the dragon fails a save, it can choose to succeed instead.
Immuun poison | poisoned
Zintuigen darkvision 120 ft., keensense 60 ft.
Talen Common,

Amphibious. The dragon can breathe air and water.

Hallucinogenic Gas. After the green dragon exhales its Poison Breath, wisps of poisonous gas laced with the alchemical taint that permeates the dragon’s body linger around it. While the dragon’s Poison Breath is unavailable, each creature that starts its turn within 30 voet of the dragon must succeed on a DC 21 WIS save or be terrified, as the gas causes the creature to experience frightful hallucinations. A terrified creature is frightened, and its speed is reduced to 0.

Legendary Resistance (3/Day). If the dragon fails a save, it can choose to succeed instead.

Acties

Multiattack. The dragon uses its Frightful Presence. It then makes one Bite attack and two Claw attacks.

Bite. Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 19 (2d10 + 8) Piercing Wonden plus 10 (3d6) Poison Wonden.

Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 22 (4d6 + 8) Slashing Wonden.

Tail. Melee Weapon Attack: +15 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. Hit: 17 (2d8 + 8) Bludgeoning Wonden.

Frightful Presence. Each creature of the dragon’s choice that is within 120 voet of the dragon and aware of it must succeed on a DC 18 WIS save or be frightened for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. If a creature’s save is successful or the effect ends for it, the creature is immune to the dragon’s Frightful Presence for the next 24 hours.

Poison Breath (Recharge 5-6). The dragon exhales poisonous gas in a 90-voet cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 21 CON save, taking 84 (24d6) Poison Wonden on a failed save, or half as many Wonden (rounded down) on a successful one.

Legendaire acties

De draak kan 3 legendaire acties nemen, gekozen uit de opties hieronder. Slechts één legendaire actie per keer, en alleen aan het einde van de beurt van een ander wezen. De draak krijgt gebruikte legendaire acties terug aan het begin van zijn beurt.

Detect. The dragon automatically detects every creature and trap within 30 voet of it, pinpointing the current location of each.

Tail Attack. The dragon makes a Tail attack.

Elemental Roar (Costs 2 Actions). The dragon roars at up to two creatures of its choice within 120 voet of it. Each target must succeed on a DC 18 CHA save or be vulnerable to Poison Wonden until the end of its next turn. A target resistant to Poison Wonden isn’t resistant to it for the duration. A target immune to Poison Wonden is still immune to it, even if the target fails the save.

Wing Attack (Costs 2 Actions). The dragon beats its wings. Each creature within 15 voet of the dragon must succeed on a DC 22 DEX save or take 22 (4d6 + 8) Bludgeoning Wonden and be knocked prone. The dragon can then fly up to half its flying speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

Balor

CR 19
Huge Fiend (Demon)
Armor Class 19 (natuurlijk pantser)
Max. wonden 292
Snelheid 40 ft., fly 80 ft.
Perception 19 Stealth 12
ResistantThe balor heeft voordeel op saves against spells and other magische effects.
Immuun fire | Demonic Resilience
Zintuigentruesight 120 ft.
Talen Abyssal, telepathy 120 ft.

Demonic Flames. Creatures resistant to Fire Wonden aren’t resistant to Fire Wonden dealt by the balor. Creatures immune to Fire Wonden are still immune to the balor’s Fire Wonden.

Demonic Resilience. The balor is resistant to cold and Lightning Wonden. In addition, it is immune to Poison Wonden and to the poisoned condition.

Diving Assault. If the balor is flying and moves at least 30 voet straight toward a creature and then hits it with a Gore attack on the same turn, that target must succeed on a DC 20 STR save or be knocked prone. If the target is prone, the balor can make one Longsword attack against it as a bonus action.

Flaming Weapons. The balor’s weapon attacks are magical. When the balor hits with any weapon, the weapon deals an extra 3d8 Fire Wonden (included in the attack).

Fire Aura. At the start of each of the balor’s turns, each creature within 10 voet of it takes 13 (3d8) Fire Wonden, and flammable objects in the aura that aren’t being worn or carried ignite.

Magie Resistance. The balor heeft voordeel op saves against spells and other magische effects.

Acties

Multiattack. The balor makes one Gore attack, one Longsword attack, and one Whip attack.

Gore. Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d6 + 8) Piercing Wonden plus 13 (3d8) Fire Wonden.

Longsword. Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 21 (3d8 + 8) Slashing Wonden, or 24 (3d10 + 8) Slashing Wonden if used with two hands, plus 13 (3d8) Fire Wonden. If the balor scores a critical hit, it rolls Wound dice three times, instead of twice.

Whip. Melee Weapon Attack: +14 to hit, reach 30 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (3d4 + 8) Slashing Wonden plus 13 (3d8) Fire Wonden, and the target must succeed on a DC 20 Strength saving throw or be pulled up to 25 voet toward the balor.

Bonusacties

Teleport. The balor magically teleports, along with any equipment it is wearing or carrying, up to 60 voet to an unoccupied space it can see.
