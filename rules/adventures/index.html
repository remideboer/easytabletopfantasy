One Shots

Self-contained adventures, converted scene-by-scene to You-Meet-In-A-Tavern (YMIAT) — Wounds and Defense instead of hit points and Armor Class, Fitness/Insight/Willpower instead of six ability scores, and the Classless XP economy for rewards. Each is adapted from an existing third-party adventure; see its credits section for the original author and license.

The Wild Sheep Chase

A wizard turned into a sheep by his own former apprentice needs help getting his hands — and his Wand of True Polymorph — back. A one-shot for a mid-tier party, adapted from the D&D 5e original by Richard Jansen-Parkes.
